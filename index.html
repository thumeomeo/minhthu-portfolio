01About

A language-and-culture student, trained by very different audiences

My path didn't start in marketing - it started with people: kindergarteners learning their first English words, Viettel staff in business-English classes, students choosing books, IELTS learners on the other end of a consultation call. Very different audiences, same job: listen first, then say the thing that lands.

Since then I've worked across marketing, community and event operations - finding the right channels, planning multilingual content, helping run faculty events, and keeping every result in trackers a whole team can rely on. That's what cultural programmes mean to me too: listening to what an audience truly cares about, then building the experience that brings them through the door.

04Why Goethe-Institut

I chose a degree in language because of culture.

Studying Russian language and culture taught me that a language is a door into how people think, joke and remember. Goethe-Institut Hanoi is where that door opens both ways - German culture for Vietnamese audiences, Vietnamese context for German programmes.

I want to learn programme-making from the inside: planning, partners, logistics and communication - and I bring what I already have: Vietnamese-English writing and event support experience with audiences from kindergarten to corporate.
